The stand

It runs on trust, which is the whole point.

Mini Barn Market sits at the heart of a working Ramona homestead. It is the community's front porch and the farm's larder at the same time, and it works on one old-fashioned principle: we assume you are good for it.

That one decision, to trust people first, is what makes the place feel the way it does.

  1. 1

    Take what you came for.

    Nobody stands behind a counter.

  2. 2

    Pay how you like.

    Cash in the box, a card, or your phone.

  3. 3

    Pull the door shut behind you.

    The door is unlocked twenty-four hours a day.
