Gift cards

A jar of something good, chosen later.

For the person who likes a farm stand, a good tomato, or the idea that a shop can be left open all night. Load a card with any amount from twenty dollars up. It never expires, and it can be topped up again.

Buy an e-gift card Ask us about physical cards

Two kinds, one balance.

Digital. Delivered by email or text within a minute or two, so it still works when you have remembered a birthday at ten at night.

Physical. A real card, picked up at the stand. We are open whenever you get here, which is the useful part.

Either one spends the same way: fresh produce, dry goods, whatever the hens have managed, and the odd thing you did not expect to find. Balances can be checked online at any time.

At the stand
